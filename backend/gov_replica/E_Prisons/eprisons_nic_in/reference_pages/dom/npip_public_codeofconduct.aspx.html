- You are solely responsible for the security of your account and password. We will not be liable for any loss/damage arising from your failure to comply with your account security.

- You are responsible for all content posted and activity that occurs from your account. Please handle your account with care.

- You must not upload, post, or send unsolicited email, SMS, or "spam" messages to other users of this site. Any kind of verbal, physical, written or other kinds of abuse of other users will cause your account to be immediately terminated.

- You must not transmit any worms or viruses or any malicious code. Also you must not make any attempt to bring the site down by unlawful activities like DDOS attacks, hacking, cross scripting attacks etc.
