FAQs
Q1. My visit is not approved or my pass status shows under process.

Ans. Pass is yet to be approved by the State Prison Department. Wait for the approval or contact the prison department directly.

NOTE: Pass approval / Status and VC date time are alloted by the State Prison Department / Prison for which visit is applied for. Please contact the respective department directly in case of such issue.

Q2. My Pass is approved but VC details not received.
Ans: Please check if the email entered in the visit request is correct. Check the registered email for approval mail. Verify Spam if mail is not found in Inbox.
Q3. Instructions for connecting VC

Ans: VC link along with approved time and steps to join the VC and pin is sent through approval email. Please check the same for details.

Q4. I am not able to connect to VC

Ans: Ensure good internet connection is available for video stream. Switch to audio only mode in case of slow response.

Q5. Site not functional or error in website

Ans: Please Contact: 24x7 NIC Service Desk at 1800 111 555 or visit servicedesk.nic.in and register complaint.
