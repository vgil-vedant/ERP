Upload documents in support of identity and address

Free till 14/06/2027

What to submit?

Ration card, Voter identity card, Government-issued identity card/ certificate having address, and Indian Passport serve as proof of both identity and address.
PAN card, Driving license, Secondary or Senior School mark sheet/ School leaving certificate having photograph, Government-issued identity card/ certificate - serve as only proof of identity.
Electricity/water/gas bill (last 3 months), Bank / Post Office passbook, rent/ lease/ leave & license agreement serve as proof of address only.
For detailed list of acceptable identity and address documents, please click here.

How to submit?

The documents can be submitted online on myAadhaar portal or by visiting any Aadhaar centre.
