Welcome to a revolutionary platform designed to automate forensic lab operations. Our portal helps forensic labs connect seamlessly with police, courts, and other key agencies, ensuring fast, secure, and transparent workflows from case registration to report dispatch.

How It Works

A seamless, step-by-step process to manage and monitor forensic cases with ease and efficiency.

  • Login

    Securely log into the portal with your credentials to access your personalized dashboard and case management tools.

  • Registration

    Quickly search for specific cases or reports using advanced filters. Easily locate case details, status updates, and relevant documents in seconds.

  • Report Submission

    Track the progress of your cases in real-time. Receive automated updates on task completion, ensuring every step is on time and nothing is overlooked.

  • Analyze & Track

    Track case status, report readiness, and dispatch timelines in real-time. Monitor case progress and key updates to ensure timely action at every stage.

  • Monitor via Dashboard

    Monitor overall case status, report readiness, and dispatch timelines through a centralized dashboard. Get a clear view of case progress and stay informed from start to finish.
